# Local SEO and business identity

Follow ../../references/AUDIT_PROTOCOL.md.

1. Establish real operating area, address/contact data, languages and authorized business identity from actual sources.
2. Compare name/address/phone, service coverage and contact paths across **observed** pages/listings; do not invent locations or hours.
3. Evaluate locally helpful service descriptions, directions where applicable, localized business FAQs and trustworthy evidence.
4. Suggest only Schema.org LocalBusiness/LegalService/ProfessionalService types applicable to actual visible facts; avoid made-up review aggregates.
5. Google Business Profile status, reviews, calls and local rankings are **not-tested** unless actually fetched from eligible connected data/public sources.
6. Never suggest fake service areas or doorway pages. Return observed discrepancies, recommended fixes and validation steps.

Official: https://support.google.com/business/ and https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business .
