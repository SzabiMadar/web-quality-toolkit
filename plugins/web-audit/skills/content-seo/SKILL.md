# Content SEO / editorial quality

Follow ../../references/AUDIT_PROTOCOL.md.

1. Define audience, market, search journey, business goal and actual pages inspected.
2. Check helpfulness, factual clarity, originality, topic coverage, author/contact transparency and natural next steps.
3. Identify specific reader questions left unanswered; avoid generic filler FAQs and ungrounded keyword targets.
4. Page overlap/cannibalization is **hypothesis** unless supported by query/page performance; do not fabricate search volume, rankings or competitor data.
5. Prefer primary-source citations and subject-matter review for legal, health or financial content.
6. Deliver a per-URL editorial backlog, short cited examples, proposed sections or corrected copy (only if requested), expected qualitative benefit and verification criteria.
7. Coordinate with on-page-seo and aeo-geo but keep editorial recommendations distinct from tested technical errors.

Official: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content .
